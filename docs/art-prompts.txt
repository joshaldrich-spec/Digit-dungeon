DIGIT DUNGEON: ART SHEETS
5 images to make in one Gemini chat. Start the chat by attaching the green hero image, then paste STYLE, then one sheet at a time.
Save each image and send them all; the game cuts each sheet into separate sprites.

----- STYLE -----
Use the attached green blob character as the style guide for everything in this chat. Cute chunky 2D game art for a kids' dungeon game: thick even dark-plum outline, flat bright colors with one soft shade and a glossy highlight, rounded shapes, big shiny eyes on anything with a face. Friendly-spooky, never scary. No text, letters or numbers anywhere in any image.

----- SHEET 1: HERO MOODS -----
Make a sprite sheet: the same green blob character three times in one row, evenly spaced, on a plain solid white background with wide white space between them. Keep the exact same body shape, color and size as the attached character in all three. Left: getting bonked, eyes squeezed shut in a wince, cheeks puffed, three little stars spinning above its head. Middle: cheering, eyes closed in happy upside-down U shapes, big open smile, a few small sparkles around it. Right: a little sad but hopeful, droopy eyes with one small tear and a small wobbly smile. Nothing touches or overlaps. No shadows, no text.

----- SHEET 2: TREASURE ITEMS -----
Make a sprite sheet of 9 game icons in a 3 by 3 grid on a plain solid white background, with wide white space between every icon so none touch or overlap. All icons the same size. Left to right, top row first: chunky round goggles whose lenses each show a tiny grid of 2 rows of 5 squares with red dots in some squares; two matching striped socks side by side; a small happy green frog in mid-hop with a dotted curved arc behind it; a large glossy pink heart with a white plus sign on it; a bright four-leaf clover with a tiny gold coin on its stem; a cute snail with closed sleepy eyes, a striped nightcap and a spiral shell; a shiny see-through soap bubble with a rainbow sheen and a small shield shape inside; a chunky yellow flashlight shining a soft cone of warm light; a single chunky gold star with a glossy highlight. No labels, no numbers, no text, no shadows.

----- SHEET 3: HATS AND REWARDS -----
Make a sprite sheet of 9 game pieces in a 3 by 3 grid on a plain solid white background, with wide white space between every piece so none touch or overlap. Left to right, top row first: a pink hair bow; a cone-shaped party hat, blue with yellow stripes and a red pom-pom; a small gold crown with a red jewel; a tall blue wizard hat with a wide brim and a yellow star on it; a round silver viking helmet with two cream horns; a shiny gold trophy cup with two handles and a star on the front; a cute pink brain character with a happy smile and little sparkles; a wooden treasure chest with gold trim, lid wide open, golden light and sparkles shining out; a blank cream parchment tablet seen from straight above, a rounded square with a thick dark-plum outline, a slightly darker bottom edge and a completely empty middle. Draw the hats on their own, front view, with nobody wearing them. No labels, no numbers, no text, no shadows.

----- SHEET 4: FLOORS AND DOORS -----
Make a sprite sheet on a plain solid white background with wide white space between every piece so none touch or overlap. Top row: three square stone floor slabs seen from straight above, each filling its own square edge to edge with no outline around it. Left slab: muted purple-gray stone with soft cracks and a few pebbles. Middle slab: cool blue-gray cave stone with a little moss in the cracks. Right slab: deep red-purple stone with tiny glowing ember specks in the cracks. Keep all three slabs low contrast and darker than the characters, so numbers placed on top stay easy to read. Bottom row, two pieces: left, a small arched wooden dungeon door with iron bands and a ring handle, shut, set in a stone arch, seen from the front; right, the same stone arch with the door swung open and a dark passage inside lit by a warm golden glow. No text.

----- SHEET 5: TITLE PICTURE -----
A title picture with no text: the green blob hero standing at the glowing entrance of a cozy cartoon dungeon, a lavender ghost and an orange puffball monster peeking out from behind stone pillars, gold coins and blank cream stone tablets scattered on the floor. Square image with a full, detailed background (not white).

----- STAYS DRAWN BY THE GAME -----
Ten-frames, bead racks, number lines, pairs pictures, numbers on tiles, half-hearts in the top bar and monster footprints are drawn by the game so the math is always exact.
