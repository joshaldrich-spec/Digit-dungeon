----- STYLE (paste first) -----
Use the attached character as the style guide for every image in this chat. Cute chunky 2D game art for a kids' dungeon game: thick even dark-plum outline, flat bright colors with one soft shade and a glossy highlight, rounded shapes, big shiny eyes on anything with a face. Friendly-spooky, never scary. Unless I say otherwise: one object, centered, filling most of a square canvas, plain solid white background, no shadow on the ground, no text or letters anywhere.
