Read this script aloud for a kids' math game. Voice: a warm, cheerful, gentle storyteller talking to a 7-year-old. Clear and unhurried, upbeat, never shouty. Read numbers and single words in a calm, even tone. Read every line below exactly once, in order, with a full one-second pause after every line. Do not add, skip, combine or repeat anything.
